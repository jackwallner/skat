Sit down at your next Skat table knowing what to bid, what to put in the Skat and which card takes the trick. Skat Trainer turns the classic German three-player card game into short practice questions, and every answer shows the reasoning.

The app itself is in German, the language Skat is played in, with the official terms (Reizen, Drücken, Bube, Grand, Null) you will hear at the table. No opponents to wait for, no account, no sign-up.

FOUR ROOMS TO START WITH

Karten & Reizen: the 32-card deck, card points, the Jacks and bidding values.
Spielarten: read a hand and choose a suit game, Grand or Null.
Drücken: pick the two cards to lay away from twelve.
Stichspiel: following suit, when trump wins and opening leads.

Flip a flashcard and swipe it away once you know it, or answer multiple-choice questions and read the explanation straight away. A short daily session builds a streak.
