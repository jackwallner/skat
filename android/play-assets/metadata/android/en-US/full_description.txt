Sit down at your next Skat table knowing what to bid, what to put in the Skat and which card takes the trick. Skat Trainer breaks the classic German three-player card game into small practice questions, and every answer shows the reasoning so the rule stays with you.

Practice on your own time. No opponents to wait for, no account, no sign-up. The app itself is in German, the language Skat is played in, with the official terms (Reizen, Drücken, Bube, Grand, Null) you will hear at the table.

FOUR ROOMS, FREE TO KEEP

Cards and Bidding (Karten & Reizen)
The 32-card deck, card points, the order of the Jacks, the bidding values and what the Skat is for.

Game Types (Spielarten)
Read a hand and tell whether it wants a suit game, a Grand or a Null. Learn which structure in your cards points the way.

Discarding (Drücken)
You picked up the Skat and hold twelve cards. Choose the two you would lay away, then compare with the trainer's plan.

Trick Play (Stichspiel)
Following suit, when trump wins, opening leads and the small decisions that turn a close round.

HOW A SESSION WORKS
Tap a flashcard to flip it and swipe it away once you know it. Multiple-choice questions check your answer right away and keep the explanation on screen. A short daily session builds a streak, and Get Started picks a quick round for where you are now.
