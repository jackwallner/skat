Skat TikTok: how to post

42 ready slideshows in Day 01 to Day 14, 3 a day, all in German. Each folder has 5 slides and caption.txt with the title, caption, pinned comment and reply. Log results in Tracker.csv.

Set up once (7-day warmup, no app posts)

  1. Make the account (or 2, for the A/B split). A German name plus "skat" works, for example Skat Tipps; keep "Skat Trainer" out of the name. Profile photo: skattipps-avatar.png in this folder.
  2. Switch to a Business account (Settings > Account), category Education. You get a bio link without 1,000 followers, plus analytics. Disclosed posts need the Commercial Music Library anyway.
  3. Bio: Skat-Tipps für Einsteiger und Skatrunden. Ich habe Skat Trainer gebaut. Bio link, account A:
    https://apps.apple.com/de/app/apple-store/id6796913722?pt=128748779&ct=tiktok_a&mt=8
    Account B: the same with ct=tiktok_b. These show up as campaigns in App Store Connect.
  4. Twice a day, scroll For You for 10 minutes. Search "skat", "skat spielen", "skatabend", "kartenspiel", watch to the end, like, follow, comment now and then.
  5. Days 5 to 7: one non-promo post per account (a deck on a table, a Skatliste). Ready when For You is mostly Skat.

Audience: German-speaking players

  • TikTok shows a new post first to people near where it was posted. Skat is played in Germany and Austria, so posts made from a US phone reach US viewers who mostly don't play. Post from a device and connection based in Germany, or expect the reach to be poor.

Each post, about 3 minutes

  1. Files > iCloud Drive > Skat TikTok Kit > today's folder. Select 1.jpg to 5.jpg > Share > Save 5 Images.
  2. TikTok > + > Photo mode > the 5 in order. Paste TITLE into the title field and CAPTION into the description.
  3. Sound: Commercial Music Library, the vibe in caption.txt, one with a trending arrow.
  4. More options > Disclose post content > Your brand. You made the app, so this is required.
  5. Post, then comment the PINNED COMMENT yourself and pin it.
  6. For the first hour, reply to every comment. Paste the REPLY on every "App?" or "Wie heißt die?". Never post a URL in comments.
  7. Always write the exact name Skat Trainer.

Schedule

  • Morning and Evening on account A, Midday on B. 7:00, 12:00 and 19:00 German time (10 pm the night before, 3 am and 10 am Pacific). Schedule them ahead in TikTok Studio.
  • Formats rotate A to G, so each gets 2 tries in 5 days. The CTA alternates inside each format: "comment App" vs "name it in the caption". caption.txt says which.

Measure trials, not just views (fill Tracker.csv at 24 h and 72 h)

  • TikTok analytics: views, saves, "App" comments, profile views.
  • RevenueCat: Skat+ trial starts per day against the average for the 2 weeks before posting. Write the extra trials in trials_next_72h.
  • App Store Connect > Analytics > Acquisition > Campaigns: tiktok_a and tiktok_b downloads. A campaign appears after 5 downloads, a day late.
