# App Store metadata and ASO pass, 2026-10-28

Scope: en-US and de-DE listing copy, plus English keyword coverage in three
additional US-indexed locale fields. App Store ID 6796913722. The app UI remains
German. No metadata was uploaded.

RevenueCat reports 112 new customers in Germany and 15 in the United States in
the last 90 days. Spain had 5, Brazil 1, and France 1. RevenueCat counts
customers. It provides no keyword search volume or App Store conversion data.

## Decisions

The title and subtitle stay as they are. In current US results, `Skat Trainer`
and `Learn Skat` rank #1. In Germany, `Skat Trainer` and `Skat üben` rank #1,
and `Skat lernen` ranks #66. No title or subtitle change risks a proven top-10
query.

The Aug 1 ASO plan and Aug 11 English title change remain in force. The Oct 13
promotional-text change deliberately sold the free tier. This pass keeps the
four-free-rooms message and the German-language disclosure in English copy.

The en-US field now uses `coach`, `offline`, and `training` for surfaced or
well-ranking learning queries. Unsupported `practice` and `lessons` additions
were removed. The de-DE field drops `doppelkopf`, which targets a different
game, and adds `quiz` and `offline`. The Russian and Chinese fields keep their
localized terms and add English terms for US indexing.

## Evidence method and limits

Search ranks are ordered results from Apple's software search API, using the
app's track ID as the rank proxy. Autocomplete is Apple's MZSearchHints surface.
The independent review rechecked the cited US and German queries and ten query
variants for each additional US-indexed locale field on 2026-10-28. All these
requests returned successfully.

Apple's current localization table lists Arabic, Simplified Chinese, Traditional
Chinese, French, Korean, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Mexican Spanish, and
Vietnamese as additional supported US languages. This pass uses the Russian and
Chinese fields for English terms. The source is [Apple's App Store localization
table](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/app-store-localizations/).

The rank checks are a query sample, not a complete inventory of every possible
word combination. Results beyond Apple's returned list are reported as not in
that response, not as proof of no search demand. These are live baseline ranks;
the local metadata edits were not uploaded, so they do not measure the changes.

### Fields

**Keywords**

- Before: `skat,cards,german card game,learn,bidding,trump,grand,null,discard,tricks,quiz,strategy,rules`
- After: `card,german,bidding,trump,grand,null,discard,tricks,quiz,strategy,rules,coach,offline,training`

The field removes `skat` and `learn`, already in the title or subtitle, and
the generic `game` token from the old `german card game` phrase. [Apple's search
guidance](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/search/) advises against generic
terms such as `game` when they add no unique value. The app is #24 for
`Skat cards`, #80 for `German Skat`, and outside the top 20 for broad
`Skat game` and `Skat card game` queries. `coach` has an
autocomplete hit but weak rank evidence. `offline` is #17 with autocomplete
support, and `training` is #1 in the US SERP. `practice` and `lessons` were
removed because neither has an autocomplete hit or a current top-200 rank for
the app.

**Promotional text**

- Before: `Practice Skat in short drills: bidding, game types, discarding into the Skat and trick play. Every answer explains why. Four rooms free, no account. App in German.`
- After: `Practice Skat with short drills in bidding, game types, discarding and trick play. Four rooms are free. Every answer explains why. The app is in German.`

The free-room offer and German UI disclosure remain visible. The new phrasing is
shorter and names the exercise areas.

**Description opening**

- Before: `Build confident Skat decisions in short, focused sessions.`

  `Skat Trainer turns the parts beginners need most into clear practice questions. There are no opponents, no timers, and no account. Every answer explains not only what is correct, but why.`
- After: `Learn Skat through short exercises for your next round. Practice bidding, game types, discarding and trick play. Each answer explains why.`

  `The four free rooms let you practice at your own pace, without opponents or an account. The app is in German and made for beginners and returning players.`

This removes the confusing `no timers` claim alongside the paid 90-second
challenge, and replaces the negation hook in the old sentence. The remaining
description body, including all four free-room details, Skat+ features, the
independent-app and house-rules disclaimers, trial terms, renewal terms, and
legal links, is retained.

## Validation and risk

`python3 scripts/validate_aso_metadata.py --locales en-US de-DE ru zh-Hans zh-Hant`
passes for the five reviewed locales. The validator now defaults to all 50
configured locales and reports 46 legacy keyword issues across 45 unchanged
locales: repeated name or subtitle terms, plus one duplicate token in Turkish.
It found no field length, whitespace, em dash, or empty-field errors.
Affected locales: ar-SA, bn-BD, ca, cs, da, el, en-AU, en-CA, en-GB, es-ES,
es-MX, fi, fr-CA, fr-FR, gu-IN, he, hi, hr, hu, id, it, ja, kn-IN, ko, ml-IN,
mr-IN, ms, nl-NL, no, or-IN, pa-IN, pl, pt-BR, pt-PT, ro, sk, sl-SI, sv,
ta-IN, te-IN, th, tr, uk, ur-PK, vi.
`python3 scripts/validate_metadata.py` passes for all 50 configured locales.
The ASO validator enforces both Apple's [100-character keyword guidance](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/product-page/)
and the [100-byte App Store Connect property limit](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/platform-version-information/)
for multibyte text.
The five reviewed keyword fields use 94, 99, 92, 94, and 95 bytes, leaving no
more than 8 bytes under the stricter limit.

Review verdict: FAIL because those existing issues remain outside this pass's
five metadata locales. All five changed locales pass the strict ASO checks.

The largest risk is keyword-only movement. Fleet history says such releases
rarely change installs, so expected gains are limited to specific learning
queries. The rank snapshots are not demand estimates. The title and subtitle
remain stable because they already carry the strongest measured ranks.
