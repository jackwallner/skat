# App Store metadata and ASO pass, 2026-10-28

Scope: en-US and de-DE listing copy. The proposed changes to three
additional US-indexed locale fields were reverted during review. App Store ID 6796913722. The app UI remains
German. No metadata was uploaded.

RevenueCat reports 112 new customers in Germany and 15 in the United States in
the last 90 days. Spain had 5, Brazil 1, and France 1. RevenueCat counts
customers. It provides no keyword search volume or App Store conversion data.

## Decisions

The title and subtitle stay as they are. In current US results, `Skat Trainer`
and `Learn Skat` rank #1. In Germany, `Skat Trainer` and `Skat üben` rank #1,
and `Skat lernen` ranks #66. No title or subtitle change risks a proven top-10
query.

The Aug 1 ASO plan and Aug 11 English title change remain in force. The Oct 13
promotional-text change deliberately sold the free tier. This pass keeps the
four-free-rooms message and the German-language disclosure in English copy.

The en-US field now uses `coach`, `offline`, and `training` for surfaced or
well-ranking learning queries. Unsupported `practice` and `lessons` additions
were removed. The de-DE field retains `doppelkopf` and adds `quiz`. The Russian and Chinese
keyword fields are restored to their pre-pass values.

## Evidence method and limits

Search ranks are ordered results from Apple's software search API, using the
app's track ID as the rank proxy. Autocomplete is Apple's MZSearchHints surface.
The prior pass reported the ranks and suggestions below. The independent review
results and its limits are recorded in the review addendum.

Apple's current localization table lists Arabic, Simplified Chinese, Traditional
Chinese, French, Korean, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Mexican Spanish, and
Vietnamese as additional supported US languages. The source is [Apple's App Store localization
table](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/app-store-localizations/).

The rank checks are a query sample, not a complete inventory of every possible
word combination. Results beyond Apple's returned list are reported as not in
that response, not as proof of no search demand. These are live baseline ranks;
the local metadata edits were not uploaded, so they do not measure the changes.

### Fields

**Keywords**

- Before: `skat,cards,german card game,learn,bidding,trump,grand,null,discard,tricks,quiz,strategy,rules`
- After: `card,german,bidding,trump,grand,null,discard,tricks,quiz,strategy,rules,coach,offline,training,game`

The field removes `skat` and `learn`, already in the title or subtitle, while retaining `game` from the old `german card game` phrase. [Apple's search
guidance](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/search/) advises against generic
terms such as `game` when they add no unique value. The app is #24 for
`Skat cards`, #80 for `German Skat`, and outside the top 20 for broad
`Skat game` and `Skat card game` queries. `coach` has an
autocomplete hit but weak rank evidence. `offline` is #17 with autocomplete
support, and `training` is #1 in the US SERP. `practice` and `lessons` were
removed because neither has an autocomplete hit or a current top-200 rank for
the app.

**Promotional text**

- Before: `Practice Skat in short drills: bidding, game types, discarding into the Skat and trick play. Every answer explains why. Four rooms free, no account. App in German.`
- After: `Practice Skat with short drills in bidding, game types, discarding and trick play. Four rooms are free. Every answer explains why. The app is in German.`

The free-room offer and German UI disclosure remain visible. The new phrasing is
shorter and names the exercise areas.

**Description opening**

- Before: `Build confident Skat decisions in short, focused sessions.`

  `Skat Trainer turns the parts beginners need most into clear practice questions. There are no opponents and no account. Every answer explains not only what is correct, but why.`

  `The four free rooms let you practice without opponents or an account. The app is in German and made for beginners and returning players.`

This replaces the negation hook in the old sentence. The remaining
description body, including all four free-room details, Skat+ features, the
independent-app and house-rules disclaimers, trial terms, renewal terms, and
legal links, is retained.

## Independent review and validation

The review uses live Apple search results as a rank proxy, not a measurement of
results after these local changes. German `doppelkopf training` ranked #1,
`skat doppelkopf` #24, `doppelkopf lernen` #54, and `doppelkopf karten` #65.
The old removal relied on a broad query and overlooked this top-10 combination.
The review restored `doppelkopf`, retaining `quiz` after `skat quiz` ranked #5.
In the US, `skat trainer game` ranked #1, so `game` was restored. `skat cards`
ranked #25 and `skat card game` #23; singular `card` remains. Name and subtitle
were unchanged, so no title or subtitle words were lost.

The independent autocomplete spot check used 20 calls. German `skat off` returned
`skat offline` and offline app listings; `skat q` returned none. US `skat coach`
returned the bare query, `skat train` returned this app, and `skat off` returned
offline listings. `skat card` returned `skat card game`. Other tested prefixes
included the app name, learn, game, trump, grand and null. German `skat reizen`
ranked #7, `skat trumpf` #3, `skat grand` #8, `skat null` #3 and `skat drücken`
#1. US `skat trainer` and `learn skat` ranked #1, `skat null` #1, `skat grand`
#3, `skat training` #1 and `skat offline` #17. US `skat card game` was #23,
`german card game` was outside 184 results and `skat coach` outside 177.
These snapshots can differ from those in the earlier pass above.

The research and live checks do not establish an exhaustive list of combinations
for every removed token, ten independent high-stakes claims per changed locale,
or fully evidenced distinct secondary-locale English query groups. Unchecked
claims remain unverified. Do not interpret older reported results as proof that
this independent review verified them.

The ASO validator now reports byte counts above 100 as warnings, as requested,
while retaining the 100-character limit. `de-DE` is 100 characters and 102
UTF-8 bytes; no other keyword field exceeds 100 bytes. App Store Connect may
still reject that field under its documented byte limit. The existing metadata
validator enforces that byte limit and therefore fails on de-DE. The all-locale
ASO validator also reports pre-existing title and subtitle keyword repetitions
and a Turkish duplicate. These are not silently fixed without local research.

Review verdict: FAIL. The 50-locale keyword rules are not satisfied and the
requested research is incomplete. No metadata was uploaded.
