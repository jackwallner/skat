# Skat Trainer port record

The app began as a structural copy of the source trainer UX and was converted
to standard-deck skat. The conversion replaces the model, card renderer,
content library, room names, primers, generated practice, StoreKit product
identifiers, RevenueCat public key, metadata, and legal/support copy.

The reusable port process is documented outside the app in
`/Users/jackwallner/cardport`. The source app's original working tree remains
separate from the clone.
