A better rhythm for Skat night.

The Skat Minute is the new daily round. Every member gets the same five questions on the same day: two hand reads, one discard decision, and two trick-play questions. Share your score, and keep a forgiving five-day weekly rhythm with a 30-day archive to catch up from.

Game Night Prep lets you set your usual Skat night. A reminder arrives beforehand and opens straight into a five-minute session built from your due mistakes, your weakest room, and material you have not seen yet.

Skat Trainer now runs natively on iPad, in portrait or landscape.

The Skat Minute and Game Night Prep are part of Skat+.
