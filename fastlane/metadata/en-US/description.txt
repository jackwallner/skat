Skat Trainer is a practice app for learning Skat, the German card game, with short drills on bidding, game types, discarding and trick play. Every answer explains why. Four practice rooms are free, with no account, and the app is in German.

FOUR FREE PRACTICE ROOMS
• Cards and Bidding: the 32-card deck, card values, suits, bidding, the Skat and how a round starts
• Game Types: recognise trump, Grand and Null, and match a hand to a game plan
• Discarding: see twelve cards, choose two to put in the Skat, and compare with the trainer's explanation
• Trick Play: following suit, trumping, the opening lead and planning your tricks

HOW YOU PRACTICE
• Swipeable coaching cards with the rule on the back, quizzes, teaching hands and discard scenarios
• Streaks, progress for each room, and an optional daily reminder
• No opponents to play against, so you can practise one decision at a time

PRIVACY
• No account, no ads and no cross-app tracking
• Your progress is stored on your iPhone

FREE AND SKAT+
The four rooms above are free and do not expire. Skat+ is an optional upgrade, as a monthly or yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. Subscriptions include a free trial for eligible new subscribers, and the app shows its length before you start. Skat+ adds:
• Endless practice with new patterns every time
• Mistake review that brings back the questions you got wrong
• A 90-second time challenge with a personal best
• Skat Minute: five new questions each day
• Skat night prep: a short mix before your next game, with an optional weekly reminder
• Extra exercises in each of the four rooms
• The Master Table: advanced discards, defence, counting game value, Null attack and rescue, and endgames

Prices appear in the app before you buy and vary by region. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Manage or cancel in Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.

Skat Trainer is an independent learning app. House rules differ, so agree on the rules at the table.
